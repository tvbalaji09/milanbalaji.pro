# Balaji’s space: useful additions

The existing page has four daily goals, dated reflections, and date-picker navigation. The most useful improvements connect those records to decisions you make each day, while keeping entry quick.

## Included in this published version

- **Daily spend and diesel:** multiple entries per day, with Add, Edit, and Remove controls. Exact integer-pence arithmetic avoids rounding errors.
- **A fresh monthly view:** daily/monthly totals, diesel totals, category breakdown, and spending average. Older months remain available. Diesel contributes to overall totals once.
- **A usable archive:** monthly CSV exports, full JSON backup/restore, and date-picker navigation for older entries.
- **Quick section links:** jump between goals, spending, diesel, and reflections as the page grows.

## Recommended next

1. **Optional monthly budget.** Show how much remains and a simple category comparison. Keep it an explicit amount you choose, with no financial recommendations or automated judgement.
2. **A short weekly review.** Summarise which goals you completed, journal days, and where spending went. Compare with the previous week without turning rest days into failures.
3. **Bring forward tomorrow’s priority — now implemented.** The reminder-for-the-road section generates the selected day’s to-dos from the previous reflection and saves completion checkboxes.

The budget and weekly review remain future suggestions; the priority to-do list is now implemented in the published version. They offer more value than adding more daily checkboxes or fields to fill out. Balaji’s new money logs remain separate from Milan’s records.
